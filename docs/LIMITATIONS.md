# Limitations

## Supported instrument

Qyvoriq targets one frontal or near-frontal circular, single-pointer gauge with a linear scale and user-supplied minimum/maximum values, units, endpoint angles, and sweep direction. It does not read calibration labels, infer a gauge's scale, handle nonlinear scales, recognize multiple gauges, correct perspective/ellipse distortion, or fuse video. A two-pointer dial is outside scope.

## Vision and uncertainty

The Hough and polar estimators share the decoded image, grayscale evidence, and circle geometry. Their agreement can reflect shared mistakes. The circle locator prefers centrally located candidates, which may fail on cropped or off-center dials. Pointer-origin and line-length constraints are heuristics. Image-quality thresholds are not calibrated from real data; localized saturation can be a false glare cue. The system has no calibrated probability and therefore does not display a confidence percentage.

`VERIFIED` means the current image passed the implementation's explicit checks: no quality flag, one supported circle found, both candidates within calibration range, and estimator disagreement at or below the validation-fitted synthetic threshold (with one refinement pass permitted after initial disagreement). It is not a guarantee that the pointer was read correctly.

## Evaluation

All committed benchmark images are generated by a small renderer with known geometry and readings. They do not represent scratches, reflections, parallax, unusual colors, nonuniform lighting, motion, real lens distortion, gauge manufacturing diversity, or site-specific capture practices. SyncG is cited but not downloaded or evaluated. No real gauge photographs, paired physical captures, confidence intervals, or independent field trial are available. Zero false accepts in this small synthetic test would not establish a safe false-accept rate in deployment.

## Active perception

The agent asks a person to capture another image; it cannot move a camera. The demo's glare/clear pair shares one generated dial state and proves only that an additional supplied image can re-enter the pipeline. It does not prove physical active perception or field reinspection success.

## Runtime and deployment

The local inspection store is in-memory, capped at 100 active records, and expires records after 30 minutes. Restarting the process loses session state. The AWS plan intentionally uses one ARM64 Fargate task and ALB stickiness; task replacement can lose an in-progress inspection. No AWS service was deployed or load-tested. Standard OpenCV 5 is used; COOL has not been run.

## Responsible use

Use the result as an operator aid only. Do not use it as the sole measurement, safety interlock, process-control input, or substitute for required inspection practices. An operator must verify readings against the instrument and site procedure. The app does not issue machine commands.
